  <div class="hero-body">
    <p class="hero-p">This ethnographic research study archive is a starting point for exploring this question. The project brings together three kinds of material: a structured collection of Sanskrit Bharatanatyam theoretical śloka recordings as the baseline for cultural adaptation, oral histories from Bharatanatyam practitioners and teachers, and a qualitative culmination of the study's findings in an ethnographic paper examining what these materials reveal about learning and sustaining the tradition.</p>
